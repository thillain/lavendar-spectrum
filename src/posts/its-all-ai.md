I am in the 20th year of my career and have witnessed many changes in the software industry. I have always held a deep passion and many dreams for technology. However, things can change significantly, and accepting reality is the only way to move forward. Don't dwell on what happened in the past; simply accept it and move on. Focus on the future, stay strong in the present, and lead a healthy and happy life.

Recently, my work has become increasingly AI-related, and our team's ecosystem has shifted significantly toward using Claude. Initially, I found it challenging to adapt to this new workflow, but I am gradually finding my rhythm. I believe that 20%-30% of knowledge should stem from human intelligence, while 70%-80% can be augmented by AI. Specifically, the core logic and business understanding must remain human-led, while AI assists with the implementation and boilerplate. This is the new reality of our work, and we must adapt.

However, we should never rely 100% on AI for serious enterprise-grade work. Always ensure there is a final human review. AI is a powerful tool when integrated into a well-structured system, but it is not a replacement for the experience of architects or senior engineers. 

Sound system design and architecture are more important than ever for AI to be truly effective. 

1. **Foundation Layer**: 0% AI - 100% Human
2. **Business Logic Layer**: 0%-5% AI - 95%-100% Human
3. **Enhancement Layer**: 50% AI - 50% Human
4. **Orchestration Layer**: 80% AI - 20% Human


## Bottom Line

| Approach | Risk | Recommended |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **100% AI** (No Structure) | 🔴 Very High | ❌ Never |
| **Traditional System Only** | 🟡 Medium | Only for simple cases |
| **Structured System + AI Tools** | 🟢 Low | ✅ Always |
